## 📊 Deck Design Guidelines

### Visual Style
**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #4285f4 (Google Blue - trust, healthcare)
- Secondary: #34a853 (Green - growth, health)
- Accent: #fbbc04 (Yellow - attention, caution)
- Text: #202124 (Dark gray, not pure black)
- Background: White with subtle texture

**Typography:**
- Headline: Poppins Bold (24-36pt)
- Body: Open Sans Regular (14-18pt)
- Data: Roboto Mono (numbers, metrics)

**Imagery:**
- High-quality healthcare photos (not stock clichés)
- Actual product screenshots (annotated)
- Simple icons (Feather Icons or Material Design)
- Minimal text per slide (80/20 rule: 80% visual, 20% text)

### Slide Layout Best Practices
✅ **Do:**
- One key message per slide
- Large, readable text (18pt minimum)
- High contrast (dark text on light background)
- Consistent header/footer (logo, slide numbers)
- White space (don't clutter)
- Data visualizations (charts > tables)

❌ **Don't:**
- Walls of text (use bullet points sparingly)
- Tiny fonts or images
- Cheesy clip art or generic stock photos
- Animations (distracting)
- Reading the slide verbatim

### Slide Count
- **Pitch Deck:** 15-20 slides (this outline)
- **Short Form:** 10 slides (remove technical details, focus on traction)
- **Demo Day:** 5 slides (problem, solution, traction, ask, team)
- **Email Deck:** 25+ slides (more detail, self-explanatory)

## 🎤 Presentation Tips

### Delivery Best Practices
**Timing:**
- 15-20 minute presentation (1 minute per slide average)
- Practice to stay under time (investors will cut you off)
- Save 10-15 minutes for Q&A

**Storytelling:**
- Start with the problem (make it emotional)
- Show solution with demo (make it tangible)
- Build credibility with traction (make it real)
- Close with vision (make it exciting)

**Presenting Style:**
- Speak conversationally (not robotic)
- Make eye contact (not staring at slides)
- Enthusiasm (investors bet on passion)
- Handle objections gracefully (prepare FAQs)

### Common Investor Questions (Prepare Answers)

**Market:**
- Why won't incumbents (Kareo, Epic) add AI billing?
- How defensible is this once competitors copy you?
- What if medical coders become obsolete (no market)?

**Product:**
- What happens when AI makes a mistake?
- How do you ensure HIPAA compliance?
- What if Google shuts down Document AI?

**Business Model:**
- Why SaaS vs. per-claim pricing?
- What's your customer acquisition cost?
- How long does it take to onboard a customer?

**Traction:**
- Why should we believe your growth projections?
- What's your churn rate?
- Who's your best customer and why?

**Team:**
- Why are you the right person to build this?
- What are you missing on the team?
- What happens if co-founder leaves?

**Competition:**
- What about [competitor X]?
- Why hasn't this been done before?
- What if [big company] enters the market?

**Funding:**
- How much runway does this give you?
- When will you be profitable?
- What's your exit strategy?
