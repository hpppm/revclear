# RevClear - Business Model & Pricing Strategy

## 💼 Revenue Models

### Option 1: SaaS Subscription (RECOMMENDED) 💰

**Per-Provider Pricing:**

| Tier | Claims/Month | Price/Month | Features |
|------|--------------|-------------|----------|
| **Starter** | 0-100 | $149 | Basic features, email support |
| **Professional** | 101-500 | $399 | All features, priority support, API access |
| **Enterprise** | 501-2000 | $899 | Custom integration, dedicated support, SLA |
| **Custom** | 2000+ | Custom | White-label, on-premise option |

**Additional Revenue:**
- Setup fee: $500 one-time
- Training: $200/hour
- Custom integrations: $2,000-10,000
- Premium support: +$200/month

**Projected Revenue (100 customers):**
- 40 Starter ($149) = $5,960/month
- 40 Professional ($399) = $15,960/month  
- 15 Enterprise ($899) = $13,485/month
- 5 Custom ($2,000 avg) = $10,000/month
- **Total: ~$45,400/month = $544,800/year**

---

### Option 2: Per-Claim Pricing 📊

**Pricing:**
- $0.50-1.00 per claim processed
- Volume discounts:
  - 0-1,000 claims: $1.00/claim
  - 1,001-5,000: $0.75/claim
  - 5,001-10,000: $0.50/claim
  - 10,000+: $0.35/claim

**Customer Value:**
- Average manual claim processing: $5-10
- RevClear cost: $0.50-1.00
- **Customer saves: $4-9 per claim (80-90% savings)**

**Projected Revenue:**
- 50,000 claims/month × $0.75 = $37,500/month = $450,000/year

---

### Option 3: Revenue Share 🤝

**Model:**
- 5-10% of claim value
- Average claim value: $150-300
- RevClear earns: $7.50-30 per successful claim
- Only charged on approved claims

**Advantages:**
- No upfront cost for customers
- Aligned incentives
- Scales with customer success

**Projected Revenue:**
- 5,000 claims/month × $200 avg × 7.5% = $75,000/month = $900,000/year

**Cost Savings:**

**Revenue Impact:**
- Reduced denials: 15% → 5% (67% improvement)
